Features

Everything you'd want from a music app on your PC

It's the real YouTube Music, signed in with your own account, plus the things the website can't do.

Listen together

Share a six-character code. Everyone in the room hears the same song at the same spot and can add to one shared queue.

Mini player on top

A slim bar floats above every window while music plays: art, title, controls, progress. Scroll on it for volume.

Global hotkeys

Play, skip, volume, like, and show the player from anywhere, even in games. Your keyboard's media keys work too.

Built-in ad blocker

No audio or video ads, no promo banners, no trackers, and "Are you still listening?" gets answered for you.

Your own Up Next

Search, paste a link, or right-click any song, album or playlist in YouTube Music. Drag to reorder.

Save any session

Turn a great night's queue into a playlist. Play it again later, solo or with friends, or push it to your YouTube Music library.

Listen together

One queue. Same song. Same second.

Rooms connect your computers directly, with no account to make and no server to run. Everyone plays through their own YouTube Music, and Wavelength keeps them in step to within a fraction of a second.

Mini player

Always there, never in the way

When music starts, a small bar drops in at the top of your screen and stays on top of everything.

  • Art, title, artist and time at a glance, plus like, previous, play/pause and next.
  • Scroll over it to change the volume, click the bar to jump in the song.
  • Drag it anywhere. It remembers where you put it.
  • In a room it shows who's listening and who added the song.
  • Choose when it shows: while playing, only when the main window is hidden, always, or never.
Your library

Signed in, it's all yours

Sign in with your Google account and your playlists, liked songs, mixes and recommendations are right there, exactly like on the website.

  • Make playlists, like songs, browse: it's YouTube Music itself, so everything works the same.
  • From your library adds any of your playlists (or Liked Music) to Up Next in order or shuffled.
  • Right-click any song, album or playlist and choose Play next or Add to Up Next.

FAQ

Good to know

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

The installer isn't code-signed (that costs hundreds of dollars a year), so Windows warns about it. Click More info â†’ Run anyway. The full source code is on GitHub.

Do I need YouTube Music Premium?

No. Sign in with any Google account, or don't sign in at all and still play music and join rooms.

Does closing the window stop the music?

No. Wavelength keeps playing in the system tray. Right-click the tray icon to quit. You can turn this off in Settings.

How do rooms connect? Does anything go through a server?

A free public matchmaking server (PeerJS) introduces the computers, then they talk to each other directly. Only room messages travel between you: song IDs, the queue, play/pause and names. No music is streamed between computers; each person plays from YouTube Music themselves.

A friend can't join my room.

Check the code and that your room is still open. On some strict school or work networks two computers can't reach each other directly; trying from a home network usually fixes it.

Will the ad blocker always work?

It works today, but YouTube changes how ads work often. If an ad ever gets through, Wavelength mutes it, presses Skip as soon as it can, and shows a countdown so you know the music is coming.

What does Wavelength collect?

Nothing. No accounts, no analytics. Your settings and saved playlists stay on your PC.
